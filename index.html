Series Trivia

Spotlight: Goku

Goku was born Kakarot on the planet Vegeta and sent to Earth as an infant, where a childhood head injury wiped out the aggressive Saiyan instincts he was meant to grow up with. Raised by Grandpa Gohan, he became Earth's most unlikely protector — and its most enthusiastic eater.

His signature move, the Kamehameha, was taught to him by Master Roshi. Goku would go on to popularize the Super Saiyan transformation, a golden-haired power surge that changed the shape of the entire franchise.

Seven Z-Fighters

A quick-fact roster of the series' core cast.

★ Goku Saiyan raised on Earth. Endlessly cheerful, endlessly hungry, unmatched in a fight.
★★ Vegeta Prince of the Saiyans. Pride and rivalry drive him to constantly chase Goku's strength.
★★★ Piccolo Namekian warrior turned mentor. Can regenerate limbs and stretch his arms at will.
★★★★ Gohan Goku's son. A scholar at heart with hidden potential that keeps surprising everyone.
★★★★★ Krillin The strongest human on Earth. Goku's oldest friend and master of the Destructo Disc.
★★★★★★ Frieza Galactic tyrant. Deceptively small in his final form, devastating in every one.
★★★★★★★ Trunks Half-Saiyan swordsman from a grim future timeline, sent back to change history.
