Goku, obviously

The one everyone knows

Born Kakarot on planet Vegeta, sent to Earth as a baby, and then a head injury as a toddler basically saved the planet — it wiped the aggressive Saiyan instincts he was supposed to grow up with. Grandpa Gohan raised him in the mountains, and somehow that turned into Earth's strongest defender who still eats like it's a competitive sport.

Master Roshi taught him the Kamehameha early on, and it's been his go-to move ever since. He's also the reason "Super Saiyan" became a thing people say in real life.

Stuff I've picked up over the years

Random facts

  • Every Saiyan's name is a pun on a vegetable — Vegeta, Kakarot (carrot), Broly (broccoli), Radish/Raditz. Toriyama really committed to that bit.
  • The Kamehameha is named after Kamehameha I, the first king of Hawaii. No real connection to the plot, Toriyama just liked how it sounded.
  • Namekians reproduce by spitting out eggs. Piccolo's whole species does this. No further comment.
  • Krillin has one of the highest death counts of any main character in the entire series. The guy cannot catch a break.
  • The famous "It's over 9000!" line is actually a mistranslation — in the original Japanese, Vegeta says 8,000. Nobody's fixing it now, it's too iconic.
  • Piccolo used to be a straight-up villain in the original Dragon Ball before turning into one of the good guys' most reliable mentors.
